Recruitment Planner

Keep the recruitable characters on your Part I path organized: see who you can pick up and what each one still needs. Story details stay hidden; deadlines don't.

Unofficial fan tool · v6.2

How to use
  1. Pick the lord whose Part I path you're playing. Each path keeps its own progress.
  2. Enter your Renown. Chapter and in-game date are optional extras.
  3. Tick each Support rank as you reach it with a character. A card moves to the top section once its Support and Renown (and chapter, if you entered one) are met.
  4. Track items, requests and paralogues in Shopping list & tasks. Each card's requirements show a ✓ for what's done.
  5. Star characters (☆) to focus the shopping list and the Favorites view on them.
  6. When someone joins, press Mark recruited. You can undo it straight away, or later under Recruited characters.
  7. Hints and puzzle answers stay hidden until you tap Spoiler.
  8. Progress is saved in this browser. Use Export backup to move it to another device.
